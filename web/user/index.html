Two ways to reach the bot. Text call drives a published contact desk turn by turn — no mic, no vendor keys, real connectors. Voice call attaches your mic and speakers to the session edge (same PCM dialect as FreeSWITCH).

Text call — contact desk

Pick a desk, choose the language you want to open in, then talk. Hindi and English both work, and you can switch mid-call just by typing in the other language (or saying “English mein baat karo”).
